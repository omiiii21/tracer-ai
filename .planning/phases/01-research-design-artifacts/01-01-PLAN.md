- Consequences (Negative): top_k retrieval default is 5 — warn against >8 (Pitfall #5 lost-in-the-middle). Defaults may need recalibration in Phase 5; ADR 006 documents this as a Phase 5 review point.

- Title: `# ADR 007: Re-ranking — None in v1`
- Context: Cross-encoder re-rankers (Cohere Rerank, BGE) improve retrieval precision but add latency, cost, and a third dependency on top of the embedder + LLM. v1's measurable goal is per-stage trace observability, not retrieval-precision SOTA.
